Engineering Mindset & Foundation

I am H K Pranjan, an undergraduate pursuing my Bachelor of Engineering in Electronics and Communication Engineering at Srinivas Institute of Technology, Mangaluru (affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University - VTU).

My fascination with technology began with wondering what happens inside silicon chips and wireless communications. Today, that curiosity has translated into writing bare-metal firmware in C/Embedded C for RISC-V and ESP32, interfacing sensors over I2C/SPI, building connected IoT telemetry pipelines, and training machine learning models for real-time computer vision tasks.

Whether competing in national-level semiconductor hackathons or building practical home automation hubs, I prioritize clean architecture, practical utility, and measurable efficiency.
